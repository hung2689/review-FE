# SWR302 Practice Hub

## Users
Students studying SWR302 who need a calm place to review source slide images, inspect OCR output, and practice verified multiple-choice questions without losing their progress.

## Product Purpose
Turn a folder of 439 source images into a local-first study and practice app. The product must protect the original wording from the images, avoid guessing correct answers, and make uncertain OCR results easy to review.

## Tone
Clear, focused, and practical. The app should feel like a study workspace, not a marketing page.

## Strategic Principles
- Original content is the source of truth.
- Questions without clear correct answers stay out of official practice.
- Every extracted item keeps source image names.
- Review workflows are first-class, not an afterthought.
- Local storage is enough for the first version.
