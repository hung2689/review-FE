# Design Notes

## Register
Product UI.

## Scene
A student studies at a laptop for a few focused evening sessions, switching between source images, OCR text, and practice mode.

## Color Strategy
Restrained. Warm off-white and ink neutrals, with one teal accent for primary actions and learning progress. Semantic green and red are reserved for answer feedback.

## Component Vocabulary
- Top navigation plus compact route tabs.
- Dense but readable panels.
- Segmented controls for modes.
- Native-feeling forms and toggles.
- Source images shown in plain framed previews, not decorative cards.
